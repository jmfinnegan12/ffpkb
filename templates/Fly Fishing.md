The index for everything fishing in this vault.

- **Before a trip,** read the technique, fish and water pages. They hold the current best advice, built from research and from the angler's trips.
- **Properties:** trips, reports and reference pages all carry `domain: fishing`, and [[Fly Fishing.base]] lists them by type.
- **Commands:**
  - `/ingest` files a trip, or advice passed on by a guide, shop, friend, book or video, and proposes the page updates it supports.
  - `/fishing-research-report` researches a water or technique and feeds the pages.
  - `/recall domain:fishing` answers questions from here, reading this hub first.

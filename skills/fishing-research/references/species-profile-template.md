# Template — Species Profile / Behavior Review

For species-focused literature-review reports (the shape first used for Elk River, BC bull trout and westslope cutthroat reports). A synthesis of published + gray literature on a species' behavior, with a defined **regional focus**.

## Parameters

- **Species** (e.g. coho salmon, steelhead, coastal cutthroat trout)
- **Regional focus** — the systems to center on (e.g. Puget Sound, Olympic Peninsula, Vancouver Island). Life-history and behavior claims should be tied to these systems where possible; a comparative section situates them against other well-studied systems.

## Research angles (one subagent each)

1. **Taxonomy, status & distribution** — subspecies/run-type structure; native range; at-risk/listing status (SARA/COSEWIC/ESA/state-provincial), verified to primary source; distribution and relative abundance in the focus regions.
2. **Migration & life history** — run timing, anadromy/residency, age structure, homing/straying, movement (telemetry where available).
3. **Spawning & habitat use** — timing, redd/substrate/temperature requirements, natal habitat, key spawning systems in the focus regions.
4. **Feeding & predation ecology** — diet by life stage, drift vs active feeding, prey shifts, piscivory; food-web notes.
5. **Threats & conservation** — hatchery interaction/introgression, habitat, thermal/climate, harvest, disease; current state and recent developments.
6. **Angling relevance** — how behavior maps to how/when the species is targeted in the focus regions (kept factual; verify any technique against regs).

## Report structure

**Frontmatter + meta** — species (scientific name), regional focus, prepared date.

**Introduction** — the species in brief; the designatable units/run types; the two-or-three conservation themes that thread through the report; a sourcing-discipline statement (peer-reviewed cited directly; guide/angling/advocacy flagged as informed-but-unverified).

**Regional / system overview** — the focus systems and where the species sits in them.

**Migration & life history** · **Spawning & habitat use** · **Feeding & predation ecology** — the behavioral core, each tied to the focus regions where evidence allows, weaving in the conservation themes rather than isolating them.

**Conservation status & threats** — status (verified), and the threats with current evidence.

**Comparative perspectives** — situate the focus-region population against 2–3 other well-studied systems for the species.

**Sources & data-quality notes** — peer-reviewed vs agency vs anecdote; dated findings flagged; conflicts and gaps stated.

## Sourcing discipline (carries the report)

- Behavior/population claims from peer-reviewed or agency technical sources are cited directly with year.
- Claims resting only on guide/angling/advocacy sources are **explicitly flagged** as informed-but-unverified, not stated as established science.
- Flag where the most recent solid data is already several years old (behavior/population age fast).
